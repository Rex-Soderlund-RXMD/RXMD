Taking the open-notebook science further than intended.

It should be a scientific epistemic aim to incorporate this for furthering science's epistemic knowledge. The goal should be to make data, interpretation, and process.

The main point is to offer a competitive edge in open epistemic territory by putting the burden on critics, people who are critical of scientific claims, to point to something specific rather than in the abstract. And so, open-notebook science should make interpretation falsifiable at the level of data, methods, definitions, citations, inference steps, and assumptions.
