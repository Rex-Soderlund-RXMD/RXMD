# Susan's Rant

**The Thought Experiment**

Everyday, a woman comes out from her home and goes on 2-hour long rant. She speaks in garbled, confusing, non-technical language that's incomprehensible to any of her neighbors. Her rants are filled with so much passion that it attracts the neighbor kids, then their parents, then the local police, and finally news reporters and Susan ends up on the internet.

Her rants become such an internet craze that people start analyzing what she says, a subreddit starts tracking everything she says on r/SusansRant, and she gets clipped across every doomscrolling platform. Nobody can understand her though, she's incomprehensible in every language and what she talks about makes zero intuitive sense.

After she's done, she always turns right around and goes into her home. She doesn't talk to anyone, she doesn't explain herself, she isn't seen in public aside from her 2-hour daily rant.

Then, on the final day, she comes out and says: I know what I've said is true, and it's on you to understand what I said. She then disappears into her house, and nobody sees her again.

The caveat is: everything she said was true, 100%, she explained everything there was to know about the entire universe and everything within and out of it. She explained every mechanism, cleared up every assumption, and declared the truth in it's rawest form. The issue is, nobody understood her.

### So, what's the point?

The point is that, socially constructed knowledge places a gamble that reliably constructed knowledge is tracking something true, without necessarily committing to a correspondence theory of truth.

Whereas, Susan was correct, but because she never offered anything legible enough to check what she had to say, the knowledge she gave was unreliable.
