Plato. The Republic of Plato. Translated by Allan Bloom. 3rd ed. Introduction by Adam Kirsch. New York: Basic Books, 2016. E-book.

---

**Why Bloom?** Bloom's _The Republic of Plato_ is best for it's literalism, and for any serious reader who intends to read the whole piece, per Bloom: the initial trouble of awkward sounding verbage later becomes rewarding as specific terms that initially seem weirdly placed, become revealing.

If I decide to read Bloom's interpretive essay at the end, it'll be read an independent essay rather than an extension of my personal thoughts. However, I did read the introduction, and both prefaces for the first, and second editions to understand the author's aims, goals, and methods of translation.
